Bonegrit’s story

Before
the virus.

Bonegrit was a normal monke, very healthy. But he was soon taken out by the virus, the one that wiped out most people.

He held onto some medication beforehand, but it was contaminated with parasites. Bonegrit had nothing to lose, so he took some to knock him out instantly.

Instead, the parasites and the virus worked together to eat around his head. Bonegrit is now blind and deaf. He gets nutrients by absorbing blood through his skin.

He only knows you're there because of vibrations through the ground.
